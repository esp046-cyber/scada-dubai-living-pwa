# JAFZA field guide for SCADA engineers

General guidance, not an official source. Station names, fees and gate rules change. Confirm with RTA, JAFZA, Dubai Trade and your client before relying on any detail.

## 1. Metro: reaching JAFZA from Discovery Gardens

- Discovery Gardens, The Gardens and Al Furjan stations are on the **Route 2020 branch**, which starts at the **Jabal Ali interchange** (RTA naming rights may show it as National Paints).
- Since Route 2020 opened, the Red Line section from Jabal Ali to the terminus is a branch. To reach stations on that side (Ibn Battuta, Energy, Danube and the terminus UAE Exchange, now Life Pharmacy) you **change platforms at Jabal Ali**.
- Typical trip: Discovery Gardens, Route 2020 train to Jabal Ali, change, then a Red Line train toward Life Pharmacy.
- Return trip: change again at Jabal Ali for the Route 2020 train toward Expo or Al Furjan.
- Check the destination shown on the platform display before boarding. Allow 10 to 15 minutes extra for the change.
- Route 2020 headways were announced at launch as 10 minutes on the initial section. Check current frequency in the RTA app, especially on Fridays and late evenings.
- Feeder bus routes change. Confirm numbers in the RTA app.

## 2. Gate access and passes

- JAFZA has several gates and each host company uses specific ones. Ask your site contact, not forums.
- Staff not sponsored by JAFZA need an access card requested by their company admin through Dubai Trade. JAFZA's guide lists a Permanent Access Card at AED 410 for one year and a Temporary Access Card at AED 100 per month plus AED 10, valid 1 to 6 months, for managerial and admin roles.
- Visitors, one-day, temporary, permanent, tools and equipment, and photography passes are issued through Tasreeh, normally requested by the host company.
- Expired temporary cards are cancelled automatically 30 days after expiry and must be re-applied for.

## 3. Taking equipment out

- Ask the client to issue a stamped outward material pass for calibrators, HART communicators, PLC or I/O spares, demo kits and laptops.
- Carry an equipment list with serial numbers, calibration certificates, and the client logistics contact.
- Client-owned PLCs, spares and removable media must not leave without written release.
- Whether goods return to the mainland, move to another site or are exported changes the paperwork. Ask the client or a customs agent.

## 4. On-site habits

- Carry Emirates ID, company site pass and gate pass.
- No photography without approval and the right pass.
- Pack water and PPE. Summer heat on site often exceeds 40C.
- Plan a transport fallback for night call-outs: Nol balance, ride-hailing app and a colleague's number.
- Test your VPN and hotspot before shutdown campaigns.

## 5. Pre-visit checklist

- [ ] Site contact and gate confirmed
- [ ] Access card or Tasreeh pass valid
- [ ] Tools and equipment pass if carrying gear
- [ ] Equipment list with serials and calibration certificates
- [ ] PPE, ID and charged devices
- [ ] Metro plan including the Jabal Ali change
- [ ] Return transport and last train time

# Bedspace and partition inspection checklist

Print this or use the in-app checklist. Visit twice if you can: mid-afternoon and around 10pm.

## A. Legal and money
- [ ] You know who the main tenant is and that the landlord consented to sub-letting. Ejari is issued to the main tenant only; informal arrangements give you little protection.
- [ ] Written agreement or message thread with rent, due date, deposit, notice period and refund terms
- [ ] Receipt for deposit and first rent
- [ ] What rent includes (DEWA, Empower cooling, Wi-Fi, cleaning) and what is split afterwards
- [ ] Number of people per room, per bathroom and per flat
- [ ] Rules on visitors, guests and quiet hours

## B. Safety
- [ ] No loose gas cylinders; ask building rules and whether induction cooking is allowed
- [ ] Smoke detector present and the exit path not blocked
- [ ] Partition does not seal you from AC supply, window or smoke detector
- [ ] Locks on the room door and on your cupboard
- [ ] Wiring looks safe: no overloaded sockets or exposed cables
- [ ] Surge-protected strip planned for laptop, monitor and chargers

## C. Comfort and sleep
- [ ] Individual AC or thermostat control works and is not locked or capped
- [ ] Ask who controls the thermostat and in which hours
- [ ] Noise test at 10pm at your exact bed: voices, doors, alerts, snoring
- [ ] Partition acoustics: MDF or drywall leaks sound. Plan earplugs and vibrating alerts if on call
- [ ] Ventilation and no mould or damp
- [ ] Hot water and pressure in the shared bathroom
- [ ] Pests: kitchen, bathroom, bed area

## D. Work readiness
- [ ] Wi-Fi speed and stability tested at your bed, ideally 30 Mbps or more
- [ ] Phone coverage tested for your hotspot SIM
- [ ] Space for laptop and monitor
- [ ] Lockable storage for laptop, dongles and documents
- [ ] Door-to-platform walk timed at the hottest hour
- [ ] Route to JAFZA planned including the Jabal Ali change

## E. Before you pay
- [ ] Dated photos of room condition sent to the landlord
- [ ] Contact numbers for main tenant, landlord and building management
- [ ] Notice period and move-out conditions agreed
- [ ] Walk away if the landlord refuses any written terms
